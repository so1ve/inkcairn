# Tools that disappear

The small tools I trust most become almost invisible during use. They preserve
the shape of the task, respond quickly, and leave behind results that do not
depend on the tool remaining installed forever.

## Quiet by design

A quiet tool does not need to be featureless. It can have strong opinions while
keeping those opinions close to the problem it solves.

The difference is visible in the output: ordinary files, familiar protocols,
and names that still make sense when inspected by hand.

## A useful test

If removing the tool would also make its output difficult to understand, the
tool may have captured too much of the work.
