# Small notes for late summer

Three observations that were too small for separate essays but worth keeping.

## Leave the rough edge visible

A tool becomes easier to trust when it exposes the real operation underneath.
Good defaults matter, but so does a clear path from convenience back to first
principles.

## Names carry architecture

When a function is difficult to name, it often owns two ideas that want to be
separated. Renaming is useful; noticing why every candidate feels wrong is more
useful.

## Keep a paper trail

Short notes age surprisingly well. The important part is not predicting which
thought will matter later, but making retrieval cheap enough that writing it
down feels effortless.
