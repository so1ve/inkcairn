# A small essay worth keeping

This fictional repost demonstrates how Inkcairn credits an original author and
points readers back to the source without hiding the local copy.

## Keep the source visible

A repost should make its origin obvious before the reader reaches the article
body. Attribution is part of the content, not a footnote added as an
afterthought.

The local page can remain useful for reading and searching while its canonical
URL identifies the original publication.

## Preserve the reading experience

Good syndication keeps the article readable, carries its source into feeds, and
avoids presenting someone else's work as original writing.
